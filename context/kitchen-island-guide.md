## What the post covers
Every decision for a kitchen island in the right order. Freestanding vs built-in: freestanding (moveable, £200–1,200, no plumbing, suits renters) vs built-in (permanent, can house hob/sink/storage, £1,500–8,000+, reads as architecture). Sizing: 90cm minimum clearance on working sides, 110cm ideal, 80cm minimum island width, 120cm minimum length — tape-test the footprint before ordering. Overhang for seating: 25–30cm minimum, 35–40cm ideal at standard 90cm worktop height; 65–70cm stool height; 60–70cm per person along overhang length. Storage underneath: deep drawers vs shelving vs open lower shelf on seating side. Contrasting island colour: the biggest UK kitchen trend — five combinations (cream/navy, cream/forest green, white/black, shaker cream/sage, white/oak). Rule: echo the island colour somewhere else in the room. Worktop: use a different material to the main run (marble island in a quartz kitchen). Pendant lighting: 70–80cm above worktop, one pendant per 100cm of island length.
