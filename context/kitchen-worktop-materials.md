## What the post covers
Six materials compared honestly across scratch, heat, acid resistance, upkeep, and cost — rated by actual daily use, not showroom conditions. Marble: beautiful but etches from acids (wine, lemon, coffee), needs annual sealing, suits people who want a living surface that develops patina. Quartz: engineered, acid-proof, zero maintenance, no sealing — but no hot pans (resin cracks), slight artificiality vs natural stone, €200–450/m installed. Granite: the hardest and most heat-resistant of all, takes pans directly, needs annual sealing, speckled look reads as dated in contemporary kitchens, €190–400/m installed. Solid wood: only material that can be fully restored by sanding, beautiful and warm, must be oiled every 3–6 months, vulnerable near the sink, €60–140/m materials. Concrete: industrial look, very high maintenance, etches and stains, €380–880/m. Laminate: massively underrated in 2026, acid-proof, zero maintenance, cannot handle heat or deep scratches, €20–55/m materials.
