## What the post covers
Five changes that transform a small bathroom without structural work: 01 Replace the mirror (oversized mirror, ideally full-width, makes the room read larger — backlit or with a shelf), 02 New tap fittings (basin mixer and shower head — the easiest visible upgrade, matte black or brushed brass), 03 Floating shelf above the toilet (dead vertical space, adds storage without floor footprint), 04 Better lighting (wall light either side of mirror at face height — not overhead — eliminates shadows; 2700K only), 05 Tile the floor only (patterned or textured floor tile does more for the room than tiling all the walls). Also covers: cost breakdown per change, what to watch out for in each. UK context, euro prices.
