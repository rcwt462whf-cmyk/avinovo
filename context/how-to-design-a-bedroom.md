# How to Design a Bedroom
**Slug:** how-to-design-a-bedroom
**Category:** Bedroom
**URL:** /blog/how-to-design-a-bedroom

## What the post covers
Five-part bedroom design guide covering: The Headboard (types — upholstered panel, wood frame, fabric, cane — and how each changes the room's register; choosing height and width relative to mattress size), Colour (earthy cocooning palette vs moody dark palette vs colours to avoid; the bedroom should feel different from the rest of the house), Lighting (ceiling light as a mistake; bedside lamps at pillow height, dimmers, no overhead light after 9pm), Texture and Materials (layering linen, bouclé, wool, cotton — minimum three materials for depth; what to avoid mixing), and finishing details (plants at floor level, artwork at eye level from the bed, bedside table height = mattress top ± 5cm). Strong emphasis on cocooning and warmth. UK-focused.

## Pin angles
- Why your bedroom ceiling light is ruining the room (and what to use instead)

- Bedroom artwork: where to hang it (hint: not at standing eye level)
- Three materials minimum — why single-texture bedrooms always feel flat
