## What the post covers
The problem: one ceiling light makes every room look flat regardless of the furniture. The fix is layered lighting — three types working together. Layer 01: ambient (base glow, ceiling fixture + floor lamp bouncing off ceiling, LED strip behind TV — all at 2700K). Layer 02: task (arc floor lamp for reading, positioned at shoulder height behind the seat, not in front). Layer 03: accent (picture lights, plant uplighters, small table lamps on shelves — purely atmospheric). Key Kelvin guide: 2700K is the only correct choice for living rooms; 4000K+ creates a clinical atmosphere. Budget to transform a room from one light: €70–€170 total. Evening rule: dim the ceiling to 30%, rely on floor lamp and accent sources.
