# Hallway Design Guide
**Slug:** hallway-design-guide

## What the post covers
The hallway is the most ignored room and the cheapest to transform. Five elements in order: Console table (25–35cm depth, 75–85cm height, 80–120cm width — no more than two-thirds of the wall; three objects max on top: tray, vase, candle). Mirror (above console or full-length leaning at the end of the hallway; opposite a window to double natural light; frame style sets the tone). Lighting (three sources: swap ceiling bulb to 2700K, add a plug-in wall light beside the mirror, add a small console lamp on a timer — total €40–€155, no electrician). Coat hooks (wall-mounted row at 150–165cm height, 15–20cm spacing, one per person plus two spare; shoes under the console or in a slim tilt-out cabinet). Runner rug (width = hallway width minus 30–40cm for visible floor edges; extend to within 30cm of far wall; low pile only, flat-weave or kilim; always use non-slip pad). Narrow hallway fix: think vertically — floating shelf replaces console, hooks above shelf, mirror above hooks, shoe cabinet on opposite wall. Weekend transformation order: clear → paint → console → mirror → hooks → lighting → runner.
