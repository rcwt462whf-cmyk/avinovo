## What the post covers
Zellige are handmade Moroccan glazed terracotta tiles — imperfect, variable, and that variation is the point. Post covers: waterproofing (terracotta is porous, must be sealed before and after grouting — failure cases online are installation errors not material failures), four main colour families (forest green, ink blue, terracotta, blanc/warm white) with what each pairs with, honest cost breakdown (entry €30–55/m² machine-assisted vs mid €55–95/m² genuine handmade Moroccan vs premium £95–150+), grout choice (contrast grout shows tile variation, tonal grout makes the wall feel monolithic), maintenance (reseal annually, pH-neutral cleaner only, squeegee after every shower), and three common mistakes (tiling every wall in a single dark colour, buying without ordering samples, using a tiler who hasn't worked with handmade tile).
