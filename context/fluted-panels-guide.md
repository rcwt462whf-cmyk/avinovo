## What the post covers
What fluted panels are (ribbed MDF/wood/PVC, 4–8mm groove depth, shadow play is the point — not new, but now accessible in sheet form). Where to use them: living room feature wall and bedroom headboard wall (ideal), kitchen island cladding and hallway (good), bathroom with PVC only (specific). Panel width: narrow 30–50mm (fine/textile, suits small rooms), medium 60–80mm (most versatile, the safe choice), wide 90mm+ (bold, needs height and space to carry it). Materials: MDF (€15–45/m², best for paint finish when primed, no wet rooms), solid wood (€50–120/m², can be oiled/stained, premium), PVC/WPC (€20–55/m², wet rooms only). Installation: construction adhesive + pin nails, spirit level on every panel, two coats MDF primer before colour, brush grooves first then roller on faces. Three colour approaches: drenched (panels + wall + ceiling same colour — the most editorial), contrast (different colour), natural (oiled timber, no paint). What kills it: more than one wall, mixing with other strong textures, wrong rib width for room scale.
