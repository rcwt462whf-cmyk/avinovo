## What the post covers
Plants die from wrong placement, not bad care — the post is structured around light levels per room, not plant species. Opens with a shadow test for diagnosing actual light conditions. Then covers: living room (fiddle leaf fig, monstera, bird of paradise, rubber plant — all bright indirect), bedroom (snake plant, peace lily, pothos, calathea — low to medium), bathroom (boston fern, spider plant, aloe, ZZ plant — humidity lovers), kitchen (herbs, pothos on cabinet tops, pilea), hallway (cast iron plant, snake plant, ZZ plant — the only plants that survive low light).

## Pin angles
- The shadow test — how to read the actual light in a room before buying anything
- Best plants for a dark bedroom (no windowsill needed)
- Plants that thrive in bathroom humidity (the ones most people don't think to use there)
- Fiddle leaf fig placement — why it keeps dying and how to fix it
- Trailing plants on top of kitchen cabinets — the easiest way to add green to a kitchen
- Low-light hallway plants that actually survive
- Why monstera leaves don't split (it's a light problem, not a care problem)
