## What the post covers
Four types of reading nook (window seat, alcove, corner chair, under-stair) with effort level and what each suits. Seven-layer build-up for any nook: 01 the seat/chair, 02 back cushion, 03 throw, 04 side table or surface, 05 task light, 06 storage (books visible, not hidden), 07 a single plant or object. Lamp placement guide: avoid overhead (creates glare on the page), adequate = floor lamp beside the chair, ideal = adjustable arm lamp fixed to wall at shoulder height. Alcove shelving as the backing of a reading nook — books as wallpaper. Under-stair nooks: the most transformative use of dead space in any home. Colour: reading nooks work best in a deeper, darker tone than the rest of the room — the enclosure is part of the appeal.
